Timing

Why Now?

Several forces are converging right now that make 2026 the perfect window to launch STAYGENIE.

1

🏛️ Daam Sakane Program is Live

Launched in Jan 2024, the program surpassed 71,000 beneficiaries by Oct 2025, with MAD 3.8B in disbursed aid. STAYGENIE is the first platform to automate the entire eligibility and application discovery process.

2

📱 Digital Gap in Real Estate

Morocco's internet penetration exceeds 90%, yet the real estate market remains stuck on paper contracts, WhatsApp listings, and word-of-mouth. The gap between digital readiness and digital tools is massive.

3

🏠 Urban Rental Crisis

Apartment prices in Casablanca reached an average of 15,310 MAD/m² in 2025. With a 28% urban rental rate (HCP 2024), the shift toward professional, AI-managed rentals is the largest growth opportunity in the sector.

4

⚽ World Cup 2030

Morocco is co-hosting the FIFA World Cup 2030. This means massive investment in infrastructure, short-term rental demand, and international attention on Morocco's housing ecosystem.

Strategic Analysis

SWOT Analysis

SWOT stands for Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats — a framework used to evaluate a project's strategic position.

✅ Strengths

What gives us an advantage over others

  • 6 specialized AI engines — no competitor offers AI-driven real estate advice in Morocco
  • Built-in subsidy eligibility checker (Daam Sakane, Damane Assakane) — unique feature
  • Serves all 3 user types: renters, owners/investors, and agencies
  • Contract intelligence flags scams and unfair clauses automatically
  • Multi-language support (Arabic, French, Darija)

⚠️ Weaknesses

Internal challenges we need to address

  • New platform — no brand recognition yet
  • Limited initial property listings (cold-start problem)
  • AI models require large datasets to improve accuracy
  • Small development team in early stages
  • Dependence on third-party data sources for subsidy info

🚀 Opportunities

External factors we can take advantage of

  • Daam Sakane program opens a direct channel to 100,000+ beneficiaries
  • FIFA World Cup 2030 will spike short-term rental demand
  • No AI-first competitor in Moroccan real estate
  • Government push for digital transformation (Morocco Digital 2030)
  • Expansion potential to Tunisia, Egypt, and other MENA markets

🔴 Threats

External risks that could impact us

  • Established players (Avito, Mubawab) could add AI features
  • Regulatory changes to subsidy programs
  • Economic downturn reducing property transactions
  • International competitors (Zillow, Airbnb) could enter Morocco
  • User trust barriers in sharing financial data with an AI
