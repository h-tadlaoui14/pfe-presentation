# Weekly Internship Report (PFE)

**Project Name / Startup:** STAYGENIE
**Sector:** PropTech (Real Estate Technology) & Artificial Intelligence
**Mission:** To digitize and simplify access to housing and real estate investment in Morocco through an "AI-First" platform.
**Context:** Startup in the development phase (MVP), aiming to solve frictions in the rental and investment market (lack of transparency, manual processes, absence of reliable data).

## 🎓 2. Capstone Project (PFE) Subject

**Title:** Design and Development of a Unified "AI-First" Real Estate Platform (Rental, Management, Investment)
**Problem Statement:**
How can Artificial Intelligence restructure a fragmented and opaque real estate market to offer a seamless, transparent, and secure user experience?
**Internship Objectives:**

- Develop a technical solution covering the entire real estate lifecycle (Search, Transaction, Rental Management).
- Integrate AI engines for dossier verification, predictive yield analysis, and legal assistance (contracts).
- Design a UX focused on trust and personalized guidance (Personas: Renter, Owner, Investor, Institution, Agency Partner).
